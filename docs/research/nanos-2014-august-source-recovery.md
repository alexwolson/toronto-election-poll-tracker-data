**Research date:** 2026-08-17

## Finding

The unresolved legacy pair is one respondent sample, not two polls:

- `toronto_2014-2014-08-31-0ebf8dc2` - the decided-voter product;
- `toronto_2014-2014-08-31-615d57f4` - the first-ranked product including unsure voters.

The legacy date is the last fieldwork day. The original report says Nanos interviewed
1,000 very likely Toronto municipal voters from **August 27 through August 31,
2014** for CTV News and The Globe and Mail. Nanos's archived first-party catalogue
dates the release **September 3, 2014** and calls it *Tory leads comfortably in
Toronto Mayoral race*. The PDF itself is titled *CTV Globe Toronto Mayoral Race
Summary - Draft submitted by Nanos, September, 2014 (Submission 2014-566A)*.

The previously known 2017 WordPress path is dead, but it is not the only original
path. A contemporary news story linked Nanos's pre-migration first-party URL,
`http://www.nanosresearch.com/library/polls/POLTOR-S14-T613.pdf`. The
[Wayback CDX record](https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=www.nanosresearch.com%2Flibrary%2Fpolls%2FPOLTOR-S14-T613.pdf&output=json&fl=timestamp,original,statuscode,mimetype,digest,length&collapse=digest)
shows a `200 application/pdf` capture from 2014-09-11, and the
[archived original replays completely](https://web.archive.org/web/20140911001710id_/http://www.nanosresearch.com/library/polls/POLTOR-S14-T613.pdf).
Nanos's [archived reports catalogue](https://web.archive.org/web/20210730210750id_/https://nanos.co/reports-2/)
independently supplies the release title, sponsor and catalogue date.

The recovered 37-page PDF was parsed and its title, methodology, topline charts and
stat sheets were visually checked. Local artifact:
`data/source_documents/historical_mayoral/nanos_2014_august/POLTOR-S14-T613.pdf`.

## Source values

The first-ranked reading including unsure voters (`n=1,000`) is Olivia Chow 21.4%,
Rob Ford 23.2%, David Soknacki 2.7%, John Tory 35.3%, Sarah Thomson 0.6%, and
Unsure 16.8%. The source-labelled decided-voter ballot (`n=832`) is Chow 25.7%,
Ford 27.9%, Soknacki 3.2%, Tory 42.4%, and Thomson 0.7%.

The report describes a live-agent random telephone survey using a dual-frame RDD
landline/cellphone sample, weighted by age and gender. It was commissioned by CTV
News and The Globe and Mail. These are source-grade replacements for the normalized
legacy proxy values, whose scraping artifacts should not be carried forward.

Acquisition does not change the sample's modelling eligibility: fieldwork preceded
the repository's 2014 Final Ballot boundary, so that remains a separate evidence-gate
decision.

## Paid archive check

Nanos's current [2014 reports page](https://nanos.co/2014-reports/) says its content
is subscriber-only, but the logged-out page does not expose an inventory. Its
subscription link redirects to the current [payments page](https://nanos.co/payments/),
which advertises several products - including a $60/year Federal Political Tracking
Portal - without stating which one unlocks the 2014 municipal report archive. Thus
there is no report-specific purchasable offer with a demonstrated price.

The current [subscription agreement](https://nanos.co/iump-register/) is also a poor
fit for a public reproducibility archive: it limits portal data to personal use,
prohibits sharing or other use without prior written consent, treats portal data as
confidential, and requires deletion after the subscription ends. No purchase should
be made for this report; the verified free archived original is both sufficient and
better aligned with the project's acquisition policy.
