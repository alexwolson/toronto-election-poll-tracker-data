# Liaison 2023 Toronto mayoral poll wave

**Acquired and audited:** 2026-08-17
**Scope:** eleven Liaison Strategies respondent samples from April 21-June 23, 2023
**Status:** eleven samples represented by twenty-two retained public HTML artifacts, twenty-two dependent vote-intention readings, and 209 response rows

## Outcome

All eleven Liaison 2023 mayoral waves are source-grade without paying for a
subscription or asking Liaison for files. Each has a public first-party Liaison
release and an exact detailed report uploaded by Liaison Strategies to Scribd.
The detailed report controls the canonical reading when the release conflicts
with it; the release remains supporting evidence for publication timing,
sponsor, fieldwork, mode and the published summary.

The public Scribd viewer exposes all 103 report pages without an account, but
its PDF download is signup/free-trial gated. The ordinary viewer HTML was
archived as an asset-dependent source lead and rendered in the public viewer.
No PDF, page-image asset, or reconstructed report was acquired. No account,
payment, bypass, or direct request to the pollster was used.

The strict five-table promotion draft is isolated at
`/private/tmp/liaison_2023_wave/`. It contains 22 source documents, 22
sample-document links, 11 respondent samples, 22 dependent readings, and 209
response rows. No shared canonical CSV, crosswalk, schema, live input, or model
file was changed.

Every detailed report identifies Liaison Strategies, the National Ethnic Press
and Media Council of Canada, IVR fieldwork, the field dates, weighting to 2021
Census demographic and geographic targets, and screening for voter eligibility.
Every report prints the same vote question:

> If an election were held today, for whom would you cast your vote for Mayor?

The all-voter demographic table prints equal total-column unweighted and
weighted bases, so those reading bases are retained. The decided-only trend
table does not print a decided-voter base, so no base is inferred from the
parent sample or the rounded undecided share.

The two 16-page reports use a longer front section: their all-voter chart/table
are pages 4-5 and their decided chart/trend table are pages 6-7. The remaining
nine reports place those four voting-intent objects on pages 3-6. Source
locators preserve that report-specific difference.

The saved viewer HTML files are 1.14-1.36 MB each and have recorded SHA-256
hashes in the draft `source_documents.csv`. They depend on Scribd's external
viewer assets and are not substitutes for publisher PDFs. Scribd's generated
descriptions and canonical metadata are also unreliable: some records point
to a different Liaison upload, and the June 24 generated description discusses
Keesmaat and Tory rather than the visible 2023 report. Canonical extraction
therefore uses only the exact report pages visibly loaded under the Liaison
uploader's document ID, never Scribd-generated summaries.

## Source conflicts and resolutions

### May 12-13 sample size

The [May 15 release](https://press.liaisonstrategies.ca/chow-continues-steady-climb/)
prints `1,257`, but the matched [detailed report](https://www.scribd.com/document/645478285)
prints `1,318` in both its methodology and total-column base rows. Its stated
margin of error is also consistent with the larger value. Under the settled
detailed-source precedence rule, the sample is
`liaison_city_2023_05_12_13_n1318` with recruited size 1,318. The release value
is retained only as a documented conflict.

### June 22-23 decided Bailão share

The [June 24 release](https://press.liaisonstrategies.ca/chow-leads-in-final-days-of-campaign/)
says in prose that Bailao is at 17%, but its structured decided-voter list
prints 12%. The matched [detailed report's decided-only table](https://www.scribd.com/document/655112063)
prints 17% in the June 22-23 column. The canonical decided reading therefore
uses Bailao 17, Bradford 4, Chow 31, Furey 11, Hunter 6, Matlow 10, Perruzza 0,
Saunders 16, and Someone Else 5. These sum to 100%, so response coverage is
`complete`.

## Reading contract

Each wave is one parent respondent sample with two dependent readings:

1. `All Voters`, including `Undecided`; and
2. `Decided Only`, excluding `Undecided`.

The exact question, eligible-voter screen, source denominator labels, detailed
table shares, and document display order are preserved. Questionnaire order
and the complete tested/rotated choice set remain `unknown`: a chart that lists
the reported major candidates plus `Someone Else` does not prove the full IVR
offer or rotation.

Candidate identities are normalized without rewriting source labels. Source
`Bailao` maps to `bailao` / `Ana Bailão`; source `Perruzza` maps to
`toronto_2023:perruzza-anthony` / `Anthony Perruzza`. `Someone Else` is an
observed `other` response, not an inferred unmeasured-candidate tail, and
`Undecided` remains a separate observed response kind. Published integer shares
are not renormalized when rounding makes a displayed reading sum from 98% to
103%.

## Remaining limits

- There is no genuinely free, durable PDF download for any of the eleven
  detailed reports. The public viewer HTML may become less useful if Scribd's
  external assets or routes change.
- Decided-voter reading bases are not printed and remain unreported.
- The reports do not establish the full IVR candidate offer, response rotation,
  or questionnaire order.
- Public access is not a redistribution licence. All raw HTML remains in the
  repository's gitignored internal audit corpus.
- Promotion into the shared historical bundle and crosswalk is a separate
  integration step. Live forecast inputs remain unchanged.
