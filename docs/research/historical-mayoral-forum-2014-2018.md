# Historical mayoral Forum wave: 2014 and 2018 source audit

**Research date:** 2026-08-17  
**Scope:** freely accessible first-party Forum Research Toronto mayoral releases for unresolved 2014 and 2018 historical samples  
**Output boundary:** source-faithful draft only; no canonical historical CSV, legacy crosswalk, schema, live input, test, or model file changed

## Outcome

Public archived-original Forum PDFs recover **22 respondent samples**: 16 of
the 28 distinct unresolved Forum proxy dates in the 2014 cycle, all five Forum
proxy dates in the 2018 cycle, and one additional exact September 6, 2018
sample that has no safe legacy proxy. The source corpus contains **23 PDFs, 24
sample-document links, 47 dependent mayoral ballot readings, and 213 response
rows**. One 2018 sample has two first-party documents; the second is a
same-sample social-housing release with no ballot reading.

The isolated generic five-table bundle is in
`/private/tmp/forum_2014_2018/`. The raw PDFs are retained under the gitignored
path
`data/source_documents/historical_mayoral/forum_2014_2018/`.

This was a public-source recovery. It did not use Borealis restricted files, a
purchase, an account, a paywall or access-control bypass, or a direct request
to Forum Research.

## Artifact record

Every artifact is a replay of a PDF originally published on Forum Research's
own `poll.forumresearch.com` host. Each returned complete PDF bytes with an
`application/pdf` media type, a `%PDF-` signature, and a usable text layer. All
**471 physical pages** were rendered with Poppler and visually inspected. No
page was clipped, corrupt, blank, or unreadable. Public availability is not an
affirmative reuse licence, so redistribution status remains `unknown` and the
raw files remain gitignored.

## Source contradictions and hard evidence boundaries

The source artifacts contain several errors or incomplete disclosures. The
bundle preserves them as provenance instead of silently selecting whichever
text seems most plausible:

- The June 24, 2014 methodology prints `June 23th`. Its five-way table heading
  and response rows say Rob Ford, while the printed question says Doug Ford.
- The July 2 five-way table heading and response rows likewise say Rob Ford,
  while its printed question says Doug Ford.
- The August 26 apparent three-way table heading and rows omit David Soknacki,
  while the printed question includes him.
- Those three contradictory readings retain all published table rows but use
  `tested_choice_set_status=unknown`; the bundle does not claim that the exact
  offered candidate field can be recovered from internally inconsistent text.
- The September 22 release omits its full current-field table. The next
  first-party release preserves a September 22 trend row of Tory 38%, Doug
  Ford 31%, Chow 25%, and combined `Other / Don’t Know` 5%. That evidence does
  not reveal the exact question or split the residual.
- The October 15 release and methodology say October 15; its trend table labels
  the current wave October 14. The bundle uses the directly stated release and
  methodology date and records the conflict.
- The July 27, 2018 release page says January 27 and its methodology says 2017.
  The archived filename, release substance, and current trend row identify the
  artifact as July 27, 2018.
- The September 7, 2018 release's methodology says September 6, 2017. Its
  release date, archived filename, and current trend row identify fieldwork as
  September 6, 2018.
- Several 2018 methodology pages contain an unrelated satisfaction-table block.
  It is preserved as part of the artifact but is not extracted as Toronto
  mayoral evidence.
