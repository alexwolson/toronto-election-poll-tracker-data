# Model the unmeasured candidate tail

Final Ballot candidates omitted from Poll Readings will form an Unmeasured Candidate Tail rather than being assigned zero support or treated as a single candidate. Tail membership is relative to each Poll Reading or evidence set, not a permanent candidate class: a candidate may be measured in one reading and unmeasured in another, while remaining distinct in every Election Outcome Draw. Candidates without validated distinguishing evidence may be exchangeable without being forced to equal shares or prevented from winning internally. The tail may affect support dynamics and aggregate forecast quantities, while individual tail-candidate probabilities remain unpublished; forecasts become unavailable when reasonable tail assumptions can materially change the published result.
