# Model Council outcomes at candidate level

The Council Forecast will represent every candidate with a Candidate Officeholding History instead of treating each ward as a binary incumbent-retention case. The history remains a collection of Officeholding Spells and Electoral Appearances—not a manually weighted score—so Officeholding Recency, vote share, margin, duration, Accession Route, and Representation Continuity can be learned and validated separately. Current Incumbent is derived from the same spell data for presentation and for defining the defeat event; it is not a separately curated model input or bonus. Competing models may learn smooth, nonlinear, or discontinuous relationships at active service and zero recency only by historical evaluation. Every sourced elected office is retained, but Toronto Council history is the core feature family; trustee, mayoral, MPP, and MP service must each validate as a separately named Predictive Feature rather than silently counting as additional Council wins. Candidates without validated distinguishing facts receive exchangeable, deliberately broad structural priors rather than editorial fame tiers or fixed equal shares. This permits current and former officeholders—and, when boundaries change, multiple holders of predecessor seats—to be represented under one schema, accepting a more demanding historical reconstruction in exchange for a coherent full-field outcome model.
