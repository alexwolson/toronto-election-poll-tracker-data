# Preserve sparse ward polling as observed evidence

Because Toronto ward polls are unusually scarce, a current-cycle direct Ward Poll Reading remains visible with its date and source limitations even when it is stale, uses a less-comparable denominator or ordinary ward-wide electorate screen, or comes from the only pollster in the ward. Repeated readings from one firm are shown chronologically rather than averaged. A Ward Polling Estimate uses each pollster's newest matching reading and weights pollsters equally; it may combine pollster-published expressed-preference denominators and ordinary ward-wide screens but still requires the same named field across at least two pollsters, and missing residuals are not filled with zero. A unique most-inclusive replicated field is preferred, while an incomparable or newly expanded field withdraws the estimate rather than preserving a misleading narrower average. Evidence-tier replication remains distinct from whether the readings are fresh and comparable enough to average. These limits prevent unsupported averaging or forecasting rather than erasing observations. Formerly hypothetical fields may become current-race evidence after every named candidate is confirmed on the Final Ballot.
