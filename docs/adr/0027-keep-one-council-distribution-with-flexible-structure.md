# Keep one Council distribution with flexible structure

The Council Forecast will produce one full-field Internal Outcome Distribution and one coherent set of Election Outcome Draws for every race, while allowing empirically validated interactions or pooling strengths to differ between incumbent and open-seat races. “One forecast” does not require identical coefficients across materially different risk sets; model-family, interaction, and pooling choices must beat the applicable simpler baselines rather than being selected editorially.
