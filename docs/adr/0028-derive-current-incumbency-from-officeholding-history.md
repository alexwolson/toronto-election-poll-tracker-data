# Derive current incumbency from officeholding history

The Council data contract will not maintain a separately curated current-incumbency predictor. Every candidate supplies the same Officeholding Spells and Electoral Appearances; Current Incumbent is deterministically derived when a spell covers the Analysis Cutoff and the candidate seeks the same office. The derived label may appear in the frontend and defines whose Incumbent Defeat Probability is calculated, but the Council model consumes common history facts such as active service, duration, Officeholding Recency, Representation Continuity, wins, shares, and margins. Competing specifications may learn a smooth, nonlinear, or discontinuous relationship at zero recency only through held-out evaluation. Accordingly, the former Council Incumbency Prior becomes the Council Officeholding-History Prior, while the separately agreed Mayoral Incumbency Prior remains unchanged.

The Ward 11 sanity check applies that one schema to both candidates: Saxe has an active spell and a narrow 2022 win; Layton has a completed exact-ward spell, three Council wins, and an older strong result. The check requires correct facts and model response, not an incumbent bonus or predetermined public Probability Band.
