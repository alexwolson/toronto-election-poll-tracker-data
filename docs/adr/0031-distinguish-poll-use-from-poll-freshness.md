# Distinguish poll use from poll freshness

An eligible Poll Reading may update the Internal Outcome Distribution only through its applicable validated choice-set treatment. At the source's recorded date-time precision, a Distinct Poll Sample satisfies the post-Final condition when any part of its fieldwork interval overlaps or follows the conservative date-time when the certified Final Ballot was publicly knowable; a sample completed entirely before that boundary remains pre-Final and cannot satisfy that floor. The Clerk's unobserved internal action time is provenance, not a predictive input. Every public Mayoral predictive quantity also requires current-cycle polling, and coarse Mayoral quantities require at least one post-Final Distinct Poll Sample. Older eligible polls remain model observations while only recent Distinct Poll Samples satisfy publication evidence floors. Age is measured from fieldwork end, not publication date; the provisional governance windows are 21 days for a poll-backed coarse quantity and 14 days for challenger Candidate Win Probability. The final windows must be frozen after the historical inventory and before evaluating 2026.
