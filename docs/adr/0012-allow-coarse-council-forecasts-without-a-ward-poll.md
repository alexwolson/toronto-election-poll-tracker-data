# Allow coarse Council forecasts without a ward poll

A direct current-race Poll Reading is not an absolute prerequisite for publishing an Incumbent Defeat Probability or Close-Result Probability. Those coarser quantities may be Forecast Available only when the applicable no-poll or sparse-poll validation and sensitivity gates pass; otherwise the race remains Forecast Unavailable or Observed Evidence Only. One eligible ward Poll Reading always updates the Internal Outcome Distribution through the poll observation model, but its existence does not automatically unlock a public forecast quantity. A Council challenger's Candidate Win Probability requires at least two Distinct Poll Samples from at least two pollsters, with that candidate individually measured in both, before its remaining gates are evaluated. These are necessary conditions rather than guarantees. This allows validated candidate history to identify serious threats while making the evidentiary limitation visible rather than manufacturing precision.
