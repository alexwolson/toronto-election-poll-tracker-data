# Retain irreducible election error

Every Internal Outcome Distribution will retain a non-zero election-day error floor for turnout composition, poll-to-result error, unresolved candidate-tail support, and other unobserved variation. Additional late evidence may reduce uncertainty but cannot collapse the forecast onto a poll topline, even immediately before voting. The size and distributional family of this floor remain empirical model-selection questions evaluated independently for the Mayoral and Council forecasts.
