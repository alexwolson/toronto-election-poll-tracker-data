# Separate poll residual from candidate tail

A Poll Residual is a survey response category and will not be treated as the combined support of Final Ballot candidates omitted from published poll rows. Until a residual-to-election relationship validates, Poll Residual affects only response and denominator handling, not candidate-tail support. A later observation model may estimate a relationship between the two, but it must preserve them as distinct quantities because historical Toronto polls show that either can be substantial when the other is zero or materially different.
