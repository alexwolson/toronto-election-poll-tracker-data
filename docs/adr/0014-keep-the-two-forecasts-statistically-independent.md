# Keep the two forecasts statistically independent

The Mayoral Forecast and Council Forecast will share no predictive variables or estimates from the other contest. In particular, the Council Forecast will not consume mayoral polling, candidate alignments, coattail estimates, or Mayoral Forecast outputs, and the Mayoral Forecast will not consume Council evidence or outputs. They may share mechanical infrastructure and official election metadata, but each forecast must be fit, validated, gated, and interpreted on its own evidence.
