# Use Poll Readings as model observations

Both forecasts will ingest the underlying comparable Poll Readings rather than substituting the publicly displayed Polling Estimate as a model input. Campaign timing comes from fieldwork dates while evidence eligibility comes from publication time. The observation record preserves the tested choices, individually published rows, population, turnout screen, denominator, undecided and residual categories, question wording, and order; not individually published, known not offered, and tested-ballot unknown remain different states. A poll using a different candidate field may inform prediction only through a historically validated choice-set treatment and otherwise remains Observed Evidence Only. Multiple scenarios or denominators from one respondent sample count as one Distinct Poll Sample and enter together only if their dependence is represented.

A full historical PDF or table book is preferred but is not an admission requirement by itself. A historical Poll Reading may enter validation when a durable source establishes its respondent-sample identity, fieldwork and publication timing, candidate field and question, population or denominator, and published topline. Source completeness remains explicit, and a reading is excluded when those essential semantics cannot be reconstructed. The Polling Estimate remains a clearly delineated descriptive product, accepting that its visible value may differ from the predictive distribution because they answer different questions.
