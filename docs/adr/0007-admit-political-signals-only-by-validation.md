# Admit political signals only by validation

Timestamped endorsements, local family officeholding relationships, and other politically plausible facts may enter a forecast only when they can be reconstructed at historical cutoffs and improve proper scoring and calibration on unseen election cycles under a pre-declared comparison. Each forecast must validate the feature and estimate its effect independently. A compelling single race is insufficient, and predictive inclusion does not authorize a causal claim. The presently reconstructed endorsement and family-officeholding corpora are not fit-ready; the facts remain stored but cannot affect v1 unless complete reconstruction and validation succeed before the model-version freeze. Facts that fail this bar remain available to the Editorial Layer.
