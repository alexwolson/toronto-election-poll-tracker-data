# Define close results by a five-point margin

The Mayoral Forecast and Council Forecast will both define a close result as an election-day winning margin no greater than five percentage points of valid votes. The common threshold is interpretable, classifies Toronto's 2023 mayoral result as close, and supplies substantially more historical Council events for evaluation than a one-point threshold, accepting that “close” remains a deliberately coarse public event rather than a claim about recount law or practical campaign competitiveness.
