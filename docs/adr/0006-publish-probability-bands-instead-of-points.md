# Publish probability bands instead of point probabilities

The forecasts will retain exact probabilities internally but release only fixed Probability Bands and their Frequency Statements. The grid is 0–<5% (“less than 1 in 10”), 5–<15% (“about 1 in 10”), successive centred ten-point bands through 85–<95% (“about 9 in 10”), and 95–100% (“more than 9 in 10”). This deliberately gives up apparent precision, avoids public claims of impossibility or certainty, and prevents the public data contract from undermining the coarser presentation, while preserving exact values for internal diagnostics and gate evaluation. Toronto's limited number of independent elections cannot demonstrate realized calibration inside every band, so the grid is a presentation rule rather than a claim of independently measured per-band frequency; exact held-out predictions and coverage remain diagnostics.

**Amended by ADR 0049.** A coarser out-of-five grid (0–<10% "less than 1 in 5", 10–<30% "about 1 in 5", 30–<50% "about 2 in 5", 50–<70% "about 3 in 5", 70–<90% "about 4 in 5", 90–100% "more than 4 in 5") is added alongside this out-of-ten grid, on the same footing — a frozen presentation rule, not a claim of measured per-band frequency. The Band Stability Gate publishes the finest grid on which the sensitivity variants agree (ADR 0018 as amended by 0049); this out-of-ten grid stays the finest, preferred resolution.
