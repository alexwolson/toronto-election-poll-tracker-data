# Validate on whole election cycles

The independent validation unit is a Held-Out Election Cycle, not an individual ward, candidate, Poll Reading, or repeated cutoff. Council evaluation will hold out complete cycles and weight cycles equally in the primary summary. Toronto mayoral polling evaluation will hold out complete elections, including the 2023 by-election as one Toronto polling-behaviour cycle, while excluding it from the Mayoral Incumbency Prior. A regular-election-only polling fit is a Mandatory Sensitivity Variant so that the unusual by-election cannot silently determine a public conclusion. The Mayoral Comparison Population will receive leave-one-city-out checks. Binary Brier score is primary for defeat and close-result events, binary log loss guards against overconfidence, multiclass log score evaluates winner distributions, and CRPS or energy score evaluates margins and vote-share distributions. Every comparison baseline is trained only inside its fold.
