# Qualify and partially pool the Mayoral Incumbency Prior

The candidate Mayoral Incumbency Prior will retain a Toronto-specific estimate while partially pooling legal incumbent defences in regular 2006–2022 elections in Toronto, Ottawa, Hamilton, Mississauga, and Brampton—the Ontario municipalities selected by Statistics Canada's fixed 2021 national top-25 population screen. It receives non-zero weight in the authoritative forecast only if the incumbency-informed endpoint specification qualifies against a polling-only Qualification Baseline under the whole-election protocol. If it does not qualify, the polling-only endpoint forecast remains authoritative and the incumbency-informed specification remains a Mandatory Sensitivity Variant rather than a forced incumbent advantage.

Toronto's 2000 defence remains only in the Toronto stratum with a three-year-regime sensitivity. Toronto's four post-amalgamation incumbent attempts—all wins—cannot identify a useful defeat tail alone, while treating peer-city attempts as fully exchangeable would conceal institutional differences. Usable Toronto mayoral elections—whether open or incumbent contests—may estimate generic Toronto poll-to-result, lead-time, and choice-set behaviour. The Mayoral Comparison Population estimates only the structural incumbency component and does not teach the forecast how Toronto polls behave.

The v1 polling likelihood and any campaign-evolution component otherwise treat candidates symmetrically conditional on their evidence. Incumbency enters only through a prior on Chow's election-day share or margin, never as a direct win-probability bonus; Incumbent Defeat Probability, Close-Result Probability, and Candidate Win Probability are all derived from the same full-field Election Outcome Draws. The prior regularizes sparse evidence but supplies no post-poll comeback bonus and may be overwhelmed by repeated high-quality Poll Readings. Chow's by-election Accession Route and strong-mayor regime receive no hand-set directional effect; Toronto-only, fixed-Ontario-population, and regime sensitivities remain internal checks, and a public quantity fails its Publication Gate when its conclusion depends materially on an unsupported assumption. A broader-Canada population is not a mandatory v1 sensitivity because it has not been completely reconstructed; it may become a future challenger only after a pre-declared audit.
