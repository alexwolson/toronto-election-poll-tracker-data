# Shift the incumbent share toward a pooled prior

The v1 incumbency-informed endpoint enters only as a prior on the incumbent's election-day share (ADR 0013). It fits the polling-only firm-balanced bridge unchanged, then replaces the incumbent's point share with a precision-weighted blend of the polling point and the Mayoral Comparison Population prior mean, `p_post = (w·p_prior + κ·p_poll) / (w + κ)`, using the fitted Dirichlet concentration `κ` as the polling weight so that strong or plentiful polls overwhelm the prior. Non-incumbent shares rescale proportionally, an open race is the bridge exactly, and a zero prior weight reduces exactly to the bridge. The prior mean is the median incumbent share under leave-one-trial-out pooling — the target election's own trial is excluded while every other trial, Toronto's included, stays in the pool. The prior pseudo-count `w` is a judgment parameter, default 8, and is never tuned to make the variant qualify.

Incumbency is a share prior only, never a direct win-probability bonus; Incumbent Defeat, Close-Result, and Candidate Win quantities are all derived from the same full-field Election Outcome Draws. The prior mean shifts only the incumbent's central share and does not yet widen the incumbent's dispersion, so the comparison population's defeat tail is not represented in v1; that dispersion is a deferred refinement.

On the four-cycle corpus the incumbency-informed variant does not qualify against the polling-only baseline: winning-margin CRPS worsens and the log-score guard does not improve, in both the all-elections and regular-elections-only populations (ADR 0030). It is therefore retained as the Mandatory Sensitivity Variant and receives no operational weight; the polling-only bridge remains authoritative. The verdict is expected rather than adverse — the only two incumbent cycles are landslides the polls already forecast, and the regime the prior is built for, a thinly-polled early campaign, is not exercised by the post-Final lead-time grid. The evidence and its limits are recorded in [docs/research/mayoral-incumbency-endpoint.md](../research/mayoral-incumbency-endpoint.md) and reproduced by `scripts/evaluate_incumbency_endpoint.py`.
