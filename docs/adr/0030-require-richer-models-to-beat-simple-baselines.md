# Require richer models to beat simple baselines

A richer specification qualifies only when it improves the aggregate out-of-cycle primary proper score over the applicable Qualification Baseline, does not worsen aggregate log loss, and improves in a majority of Held-Out Election Cycles. Otherwise the simpler specification remains authoritative. For Mayoral model-family qualification, the primary score is winning-margin CRPS and the overconfidence guard is winner log score: winning margin is continuous, exists in every reconstructed cycle, and directly underlies the settled Close-Result event without allowing dozens of tiny tail candidates to dilute the comparison. Mean candidate-share CRPS remains a diagnostic; Close-Result, Incumbent Defeat, and named Candidate Win quantities retain their separate event-specific Brier and log-loss checks and evidence gates.

The Mayoral Forecast's initial polling-only Qualification Baseline is a symmetric Mayoral Endpoint Bridge that learns poll-to-result uncertainty without modelling a latent daily campaign path. The bridge must itself clear frozen broad reliability and overconfidence checks against a pre-declared simple polling comparator before any Mayoral predictive quantity may publish; being the best tested specification is not sufficient. A dynamic state-space specification may become authoritative only by beating that endpoint baseline under this rule; no existing NumPyro, Markov-chain, or other computational toolkit is presumed. This is a deliberately conservative governance rule rather than a conventional significance test, which the small number of independent Toronto elections cannot support.

Endpoint qualification uses the common post-Final lead-time grid 12, 7, 3, and 1 days before election day, evaluated at 12:00 America/Toronto on each cutoff date. Every reconstructed Toronto cycle has audited poll evidence at those cutoffs. A polling-only endpoint with no eligible Poll Reading fails that cutoff rather than manufacturing a uniform forecast; earlier cutoffs may be added only in a later model version with a real non-poll structural baseline.
