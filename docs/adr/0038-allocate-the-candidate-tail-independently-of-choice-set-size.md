# Allocate the candidate tail independently of choice-set size

The Mayoral Endpoint allocates Unmeasured Candidate Tail mass as one total omitted mass per election, independent of how many Final-Ballot candidates a Poll Reading happens to omit, rather than as a per-omitted-candidate odds that grows the total tail with the count of omitted names. The fit learns one realized total tail per training cycle and averages across cycles with equal weight; a reading omitting `m` candidates spreads that total evenly among its `m` omitted candidates. Tail candidates remain distinct in every Election Outcome Draw and undistinguished candidates stay exchangeable (ADR 0004), and a Poll Residual is still never converted into candidate support (ADR 0010).

This replaces the earlier per-candidate count-scaling, which credited each omitted candidate a fixed odds through a `1 + m·odds` normalizer and therefore predicted more total tail whenever a poll named fewer candidates. On the reconstructed Toronto corpus that relationship is absent: realized total omitted mass does not rise with the number of omitted candidates, and under leave-one-cycle-out the count-scaling model over-predicts tail mass on the two large-field cycles by 12 to 16 points of valid-vote share, while a choice-set-size-invariant total roughly halves the held-out tail-mass error. The evidence is recorded in [docs/research/candidate-tail-choice-set-scaling.md](../research/candidate-tail-choice-set-scaling.md) and reproduced by `scripts/diagnose_candidate_tail_scaling.py`.

The change is adopted because count-scaling was empirically unjustified, not because a fixed total is a proven law: with only four independent cycles and `m` confounded with ballot size, this rules out scaling the tail by omitted-candidate count more confidently than it endorses any particular replacement, so the flat total is preferred as the minimal specification over a shrunk ballot-size term that four cycles cannot identify. Tail calibration is evaluated separately from headline performance; the constant-total endpoint must still clear the frozen winning-margin and log-score qualification checks (ADR 0030) before any Mayoral quantity publishes, and those metrics are reported, not tuned toward.
