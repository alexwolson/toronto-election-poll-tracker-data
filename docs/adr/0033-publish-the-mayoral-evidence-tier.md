# Publish the Mayoral evidence tier

Every public Mayoral quantity will display its current-cycle polling evidence tier separately from Forecast Available or Forecast Unavailable: `M0 — Structural Only`, `M1 — Pre-Final Polling`, `M2 — Post-Final Polling`, or `M3 — Replicated Post-Final Polling`. No Mayoral predictive quantity may publish without qualifying current-cycle polling. At the source's recorded date-time precision, a sample counts as post-Final when its fieldwork interval overlaps or follows the conservative date-time when the certified Final Ballot was publicly knowable; a sample completed entirely before that boundary remains pre-Final. M2 requires at least one post-Final Distinct Poll Sample before Incumbent Defeat Probability or Close-Result Probability becomes eligible. For a challenger's Candidate Win Probability, M3 requires at least three Distinct Poll Samples across at least two pollsters, at least two post-Final, with that challenger individually measured in all three. These floors establish eligibility only; every other Publication Gate still applies.
