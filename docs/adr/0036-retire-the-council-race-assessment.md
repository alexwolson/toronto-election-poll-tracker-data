# Retire the Council race assessment

The rebuilt Council product will not classify races as Safe, Competitive, or Open through a rule-based model assessment. Open Seat remains a factual race property, while modelled competitiveness is expressed only through gated Incumbent Defeat Probability and Close-Result Probability; when those quantities are unavailable, dated Poll Readings remain Observed Evidence Only without a derived polling verdict, and journalistic characterization belongs to the independent Editorial Layer.
