# Launch Council with race-level quantities

The first rebuilt Council Forecast will publish only race-level quantities and will not include a council-wide composition forecast. Toronto Council is non-partisan, so ideological composition would require an editorial taxonomy, while even a mechanical incumbent-return aggregate needs a separately validated cross-ward dependence model and its own Publication Gates. A later version may add a mechanical aggregate such as current incumbents returned or defeated without conditioning it on the Mayoral Forecast.
